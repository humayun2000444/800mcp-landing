Questions

What people ask before they tap.

Is it free — even from abroad?

Yes. The call runs over the internet from your browser, so it is toll-free from anywhere in the world: no call charges, no international rates, no roaming — only your usual data. Looking around and booking a viewing cost nothing either.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It works in the browser of any modern phone: type the address on the sign — 800mcp.com — and the call opens.

Are the cameras really live?

Yes. What you see is the apartment as it is at that moment, room by room — not photographs.

Who answers the call?

Our assistant, straight away, in English or Arabic. Ask for a person at any time and one of our property consultants will call you.

Will someone try to sell me something?

No. Look around as long as you like and hang up whenever you want. We call you only if you leave your number and ask us to.

What happens to my number?

We use your name and number only to arrange your viewing and to call you about the property. Nothing else.

Which towers can I see?

Any tower with an 800mcp sign — from in front of it, or from home, anywhere in the world. Start a live preview and tell the assistant which tower you mean.

Who pays for 800mcp?

Never the caller: it is toll-free from anywhere in the world. Property owners and developers pay to put their towers on 800mcp, by quotation — write to support@800mcp.com.
